Calibration workflow

1. Point the camera at your target and set exposure, gain and frame rate.
2. Disable Auto levels to compare brightness consistently; adjust black and white levels.
3. Use a neutral target for white balance. Manual RGB multipliers affect the preview only.
4. Save calibration to restore these settings after a service restart.
